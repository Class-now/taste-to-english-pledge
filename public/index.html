                How it works

                • Qloo's taste graph finds cross-domain matches — a Japanese game can lead to an English podcast.
                • An AI agent estimates English difficulty, checks its own picks (if a format comes up short, it asks Qloo again with your taste tags) and writes the plan.
                • Every step and tool call is shown live in the agent trace.
                • Only the titles you type are sent to Qloo and the AI, so please don't enter personal details. Nothing is stored except a 24-hour cache of Qloo results; there are no accounts.

                Limits

                • Qloo results are aggregate taste patterns, not about you.
                • CEFR levels are estimates. If the AI is unavailable, simpler rule-based estimates are used and marked.
                • Demo mode uses a small hand-written sample set, not real Qloo data.
